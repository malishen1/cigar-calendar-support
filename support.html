Cigar Calendar: Support

Cigar Calendar is a private collection journal for cigar lovers: log sessions, manage your humidor, track humidity and see your year in cigars.

Contact us

For questions, problems or feedback, email sales@matzhis.com. We reply within two working days.

Delete your account

In the app, tap the profile icon, open Settings and choose Delete Account, then confirm. Your account and its journal, humidor, humidity readings, community content and calendar links are permanently removed.

Apple Calendar

Signed-in users can open Profile and tap Add to Apple Calendar. Your sessions then appear in the Calendar app on iPhone, iPad and Mac and update automatically. Turn the link off from the same screen and remove the subscribed calendar in the Calendar app.

Report content

Tap the flag icon on any community post or comment, or email us. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours and users who break the rules are removed.

Community guidelines

No objectionable, offensive or abusive content. The app is for adults (18+) only.
