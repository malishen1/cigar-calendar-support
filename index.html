Questions

Before you download

Is it free?

Cigar Calendar is free to download. You can start logging straight away in guest mode.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest mode works from the first launch, so you can start logging straight away. Create an account later if you want one.

Is my data private?

Your log is yours. What is stored and why is explained in our privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. That removes your account and the data attached to it. If you get stuck, support will help.
