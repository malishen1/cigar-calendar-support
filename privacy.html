Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Without an account

You can use the journal, humidor and humidity log without an account. That data is stored only on your device.

Your account and journal

If you create an account we store your username, email address, a password hash, your journal entries, humidor and humidity readings and the account creation date, so your data syncs across your devices. Session cookies keep you signed in.

Photos

Photos you add to journal entries stay on your device and are never uploaded. They only leave it if you include them in an export you share yourself.

Community

The community is optional. We record the version and date of the terms you accept. Approved posts, comments, images and your username are visible to other members. Reports and blocks are stored for moderation.

Optional AI

If you choose an AI scan or recommendations, the selected image or history is sent through our server to OpenAI to process that request (OpenAI privacy policy). Manual entry works without AI.

Calendars

Google Calendar is optional; if connected, an encrypted authorization token is stored to create, update and remove the entries you request. Apple Calendar is optional; if you turn it on, we create a private feed link that lets your Calendar app read your session titles, dates, ratings and notes. Only a hashed version of the link's secret is stored, and you can turn the link off at any time.

Service providers

The app is hosted on Render (application server and database). Email is used only to send verification and password-reset codes.

No tracking or advertising

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

Deletion

Profile → Settings → Delete Account removes your account and all associated data from our database. Calendar events already copied into external calendars are managed in those apps.
